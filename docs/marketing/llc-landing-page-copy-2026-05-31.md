## Problem section

### Section headline
**You love science. Pulling it together is the exhausting part.**

### Body copy
For one science lesson, you end up stitching together videos, printables, library books, supply lists, and half-finished ideas from five different places.

And even then, it still might be:
- too much prep
- too dependent on screens
- too classroom-ish to work well at home
- too vague to teach confidently
- too complicated to repeat next week

That’s not a science problem.
That’s a curriculum design problem.
