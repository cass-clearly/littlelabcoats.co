Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Little Lab Coats respects your privacy. This page explains the information we receive when you contact us or buy a digital product and how we use it.

Information we receive

We receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and message when you contact us. When you make a purchase, our payment processor, Stripe, handles your payment details. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.

How we use information

We use contact and order information to respond to questions, provide purchased digital materials, support customers, and improve Little Lab Coats. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing information

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the site and deliver purchases, such as Stripe for payment processing, or when required by law.

Children's privacy

Little Lab Coats materials are made for families and educators. Purchases and contact requests are intended to be made by adults.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact hello@littlelabcoats.com.
