Grade 6 Enquiry Labs — Quiz

Name: __________________ Date: __________

  1. For a friction investigation, name the independent variable and two controls.
  2. Why are repeated trials more useful than one trial?
  3. In an osmosis investigation, explain why a potato strip might change mass.
  4. Sort these: weathering, erosion, deposition. Which one moves sediment?

Stretch

A result disagrees with your hypothesis. What should a scientist do next? Give two evidence-based actions rather than changing the data.
