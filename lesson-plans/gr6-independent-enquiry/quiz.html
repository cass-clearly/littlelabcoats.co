🧪 Grade 6 Independent Enquiry Labs Quiz 🧪

Evidence, models, and scientific explanations

Use this after the four investigations. Students should explain answers with evidence, not just select a vocabulary word.

Multiple Choice

  1. Which is the best control in a fair-test investigation?
    A. A variable you change on purpose B. A condition kept the same C. The result you want D. A surprising result
  2. Why are repeated trials useful?
    A. They make a graph look full B. They help reveal a pattern and identify outliers C. They guarantee the hypothesis is right D. They remove all uncertainty
  3. Which process moves sediment from one place to another?
    A. Weathering B. Erosion C. Deposition D. Osmosis
  4. In osmosis, water moves across a selectively permeable membrane toward the side with:
    A. fewer dissolved particles B. more dissolved particles C. no cells D. more gravity
  5. Which statement about an insulated ice cup is accurate?
    A. Cold flows outward B. Thermal energy moves from warmer surroundings toward cooler ice C. Insulation stops all energy transfer D. Foil always wins

✍️ Short Response

Choose one lab. Write a claim, two pieces of evidence, and reasoning that uses a science concept from the lesson.

✂️ PARENT ANSWER KEY — Detach before giving worksheet to students

Answers: 1-B, 2-B, 3-B, 4-B, 5-B.

Short response: Look for a clear claim, two specific data points or observations, and reasoning that accurately uses a relevant science concept. A different conclusion is acceptable when it is supported by the student’s own evidence.
