Grade 6 Independent Enquiry Labs

Four investigations. Your question, your evidence, your explanation.

These labs give you a scientific problem and safe boundaries—not a script. A good scientist decides what must stay the same, repeats trials, and lets evidence change their mind.

Scientific enquiry routine: Ask a testable question → predict → identify variables and controls → design fair trials → organize data → graph a pattern → write a CER → improve the next test.
