Friction Track Detective

Grade 6 Quick Reference

Big Idea
Friction is a contact force that pushes against motion. When the same toy car rolls across different surfaces, the surface can change how quickly the car slows down.

Key Vocabulary

force · friction · contact force · variable · control · trial · mean · evidence

Use Evidence Like a Scientist

  • State the pattern in your data.
  • Use at least two specific measurements.
  • Explain the pattern with the science idea.
  • Name one limitation or next test.
